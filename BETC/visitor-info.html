Visitor Information

BETC 2027 — Leuven, Belgium

Belgian Empirical Trade Conference · June 17–18, 2027
Oude Markt, Leuven
KU Leuven University Library, Leuven

Venue

KU Leuven

The conference will be hosted by KU Leuven in Leuven, Belgium. The precise venue will be announced.

Getting there

By air

Brussels Airport (BRU) is the closest international airport, roughly 20 km from Leuven. Direct trains run from the station beneath the airport terminal to Leuven several times an hour, with a journey time of about 15 minutes. A small airport supplement is added to train tickets to and from Brussels Airport.

Brussels South Charleroi Airport (CRL), served mainly by low-cost carriers, is considerably farther away. Travel from Charleroi to Leuven by shuttle bus and train takes about two hours.

By train

High-speed trains from London, Paris, Amsterdam, and Germany arrive at Brussels-South (Brussel-Zuid / Bruxelles-Midi). Frequent trains connect Brussels to Leuven, taking about 30 minutes. Timetables and tickets are available from the Belgian national railway, SNCB/NMBS.

From Leuven station

Leuven railway station is at the edge of the historic center. The Grote Markt is about a 15-minute walk away, and buses run frequently from the station into town.

Local transportation

Walking

Leuven's historic center is compact, and most places of interest are within easy walking distance of one another.

Bus

Local and regional buses are operated by De Lijn. Timetables and ticket options are available on the De Lijn website and app.

Cycling

Leuven is a cycling city with an extensive network of bike lanes. Bicycles can be rented at several locations in town, including at the railway station.

Practical information

Currency

Euro (€). Credit and debit cards are widely accepted, and contactless payment is standard in shops, restaurants, and on public transport.

Language

Leuven lies in the Dutch-speaking region of Flanders. English is widely spoken, particularly at the university, in hotels, and in restaurants.

Weather

Mid-June in Belgium is typically mild, with daytime temperatures around 18–23°C (64–73°F) and cooler evenings. Rain showers are common at any time of year, so an umbrella or light rain jacket is recommended.

Entry requirements

Belgium is part of the Schengen Area. Check with the Belgian Federal Public Service Foreign Affairs for the latest entry requirements for your nationality.

Power supply

Belgium uses Type E plugs (230V, 50Hz); Type C plugs also fit. Visitors from North America, the UK, and Australia will need an adapter.

Emergency numbers

European emergency number: 112

About Leuven

Leuven is a historic university town about 25 km east of Brussels. Its university, founded in 1425, is one of the oldest in Europe, and students give the town much of its character. The late-Gothic Town Hall on the Grote Markt, with its richly decorated façade, and the adjacent St. Peter's Church are the town's best-known landmarks.

The University Library on Ladeuzeplein, rebuilt after the First World War, is topped by a tower that visitors can climb for views over the town. The Groot Begijnhof, a walled quarter of brick houses and cobbled lanes, is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The Oude Markt, lined with cafés and bars, is the center of Leuven's social life, and the town has a long brewing tradition.
