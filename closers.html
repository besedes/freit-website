The closers

Giving the last paper of a conference is often a thankless job, as some participants need to leave before the end. Since 1995, 78 economists have given the closing paper at 89 FREIT conferences. We thank all of them. More on how FREIT conferences came about is in the history of FREIT, and the conferences’ keynote speakers are acknowledged on a separate page.
